Place downloaded cover ("coverhead") videos in this folder.

See ../../README.md for the full list of Pexels video IDs and the direct download pattern:

  https://videos.pexels.com/video-files/<ID>/<ID>-hd_1920_1080_25fps.mp4

Suggested naming:
  home-health-hero.mp4      (Pexels 7475237)
  home-health-intro.mp4     (Pexels 7475239)
  hospice-hero.mp4          (Pexels 7522364)
  hospice-intro.mp4         (Pexels 8057825)
  private-duty-hero.mp4     (Pexels 7517374)
  private-duty-intro.mp4    (Pexels 7517387)

Encode each one for the web before shipping (see README.md for ffmpeg commands).
Target: under ~6 MB, 12-20 seconds, no audio track, +faststart.
