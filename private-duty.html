Free assessment

Start with a conversation, not a contract.

We will come to the home, meet your loved one, look honestly at what is and is not working, and tell you what level of support we think is needed — including if that is less than you expected, or a different service altogether.

What the assessment covers

  • Current ability with bathing, dressing, mobility, meals and medication.
  • Safety of the home environment and fall risk.
  • Existing family and community support, and where the gaps are.
  • Whether hourly visits or live-in care is the better fit.
  • A clear recommendation, schedule and rate before anything is agreed.

In-home care services — hourly or live-in

Everything our caregivers can help with.

Care plans are built from the tasks below and adjusted as needs change. Choose a few hours a week, full days, overnight cover, or live-in support.

01Bathing & personal hygiene

Safe assistance with bathing, showering, oral care and skin care, at the pace the client prefers.

02Dressing & grooming

Help choosing and putting on clothing, plus hair, shaving and nail care so clients look and feel like themselves.

03Toileting & incontinence care

Discreet, respectful assistance with toileting, commode use and incontinence care.

04Meal planning & preparation

Shopping for and cooking meals that match dietary needs and personal taste — and sitting down to share them.

05Feeding assistance

Patient help with eating and drinking, including monitoring hydration and appetite.

06Medication reminders

Timely prompts to take prescribed medication as directed, with changes reported to the family.

07Mobility, transfers & walking

Assistance moving safely around the home, in and out of bed or chairs, and with prescribed walking.

08Light housekeeping & laundry

Tidying, dishes, bed changing, laundry and keeping the living space clean and hazard-free.

09Errands, shopping & transportation

Running errands, collecting prescriptions and accompanying clients to appointments and outings.

10Companionship & engagement

Conversation, games, hobbies, walks and a reassuring presence — often the part families value most.

Respite for family caregivers

Family members providing care around the clock need breaks too. Our caregivers step in for a few hours, a weekend or longer so you can rest, work or travel knowing your loved one is in capable hands.

Our caregivers

Who we let into your home.

We hire for character first and then train hard for competence. Every caregiver who works with an Assist on Call client meets all of the following before their first shift.

  • Thoroughly screened, interviewed and reference-checked before hire.
  • Criminal background check and fingerprint clearance completed.
  • Registered Home Care Aides in accordance with California requirements.
  • Current TB clearance and health screening on file.
  • CPR and first aid certified.
  • Bonded and insured, with workers’ compensation coverage in place.
  • Oriented, trained and supervised on an ongoing basis by our office team.
