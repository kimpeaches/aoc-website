Requirements

What we look for.

Our private duty caregivers provide hourly and live-in support with personal care, meals, medication reminders, housekeeping, errands and companionship.

  • Registered, or willing to register, as a Home Care Aide in California.
  • Experience caring for older adults or people with disabilities.
  • Current CPR and first aid certification (or willingness to obtain it).
  • Current TB clearance.
  • Criminal background check and fingerprint clearance.
  • Reliable transportation and a valid driver’s licence where driving is required.
  • Legal authorisation to work in the United States.
  • Patience, dependability, discretion and genuine warmth.
  • Ability to follow a care plan and report changes accurately.
  • Availability for hourly shifts, overnights or live-in placements.
