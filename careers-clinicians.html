Requirements

What we look for.

We hire registered nurses, licensed vocational nurses, physical, occupational and speech therapists and assistants, medical social workers and certified home health aides.

  • A current, valid and unencumbered California professional licence or certification for your discipline (RN, LVN, PT, PTA, OT, COTA, SLP, MSW, CHHA).
  • Proof of graduation from an accredited programme for your discipline.
  • At least one year of relevant clinical experience is preferred, home health experience especially so.
  • Current CPR certification.
  • Current TB clearance and a physical examination clearance.
  • Criminal background check and fingerprint clearance.
  • Valid California driver’s licence, reliable transportation and current auto insurance.
  • Legal authorisation to work in the United States.
  • Strong assessment, documentation and communication skills.
  • Compassion, reliability and a genuine commitment to care delivered in the home.
