Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

California residents may direct us not to sell or share their personal information. Assist on Call does not sell personal information — and you may still submit a request below to have that confirmed in writing.

Our position

Assist on Call does not sell personal information, and does not share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. Patient medical information is additionally protected under HIPAA and California medical privacy law.

Submitting a request

You may submit an opt-out or information request by calling 925-969-7634, by emailing info@assistoncallprof.com, or by completing the form below. An authorised agent may submit a request on your behalf with proof of authorisation.

We will acknowledge your request and respond within the timeframes required by law. We may need to verify your identity first, and we will only use the information you provide for that purpose.

Please do not include medical details

This form is for privacy requests only. Do not include diagnoses, treatment details or other health information here. To discuss medical records, call 925-969-7634.
