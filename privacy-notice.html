Privacy Notice

This notice explains what personal information Assist on Call collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have.

  1. Home
  2. Privacy Notice

Before you publish

This page reproduces the structure and substance of the original privacy notice. Have your compliance officer or attorney review and confirm the final wording — particularly the HIPAA, CCPA and retention sections — and insert your effective date before going live.

Who this notice applies to

This notice applies to Assist on Call Professional In-Home Care Services, Inc., Assist on Call Palliative & Hospice Care, Inc., and Assist on Call, Inc., all located at 2100 Monument Blvd., Pleasant Hill, CA 94523.

It covers information collected through this website, through our forms, by telephone, and in the course of providing care.

Information we collect

Identifiers such as your name, postal address, email address, telephone and fax numbers.

Health and medical information, including diagnoses, physician orders, plans of care, medications and clinical notes, where you are a patient or a person being referred for care.

Insurance and payer information necessary to verify coverage and bill for services.

Employment information such as résumés, licence and certification numbers, references and background-check results, where you apply to work with us.

Internet and device information such as IP address, browser type, pages viewed and referring pages, collected automatically when you use this website.

How we use your information

To assess needs, arrange and deliver care, and coordinate with your physicians and other providers.

To verify insurance coverage, obtain authorisations and bill for services rendered.

To respond to your enquiries, appointment requests and referrals.

To evaluate applications for employment and to meet licensing, screening and training requirements.

To operate, secure and improve this website.

To comply with legal, regulatory, accreditation and public-health obligations.

How we share your information

With physicians, hospitals, laboratories, pharmacies, equipment suppliers and other providers involved in your care.

With health plans, Medicare, Medi-Cal and other payers for coverage verification, authorisation and payment.

With service providers who perform functions on our behalf under written agreements limiting their use of the information.

With regulators, licensing bodies, accrediting organisations and law enforcement where required or permitted by law.

We do not sell your personal information. See our Do Not Sell or Share page for details.

Protected health information and HIPAA

Medical information about our patients is protected health information governed by HIPAA and California law. Our separate Notice of Privacy Practices describes those protections and your rights in detail, and is provided to every patient at the start of care. A copy is available on request by calling 925-969-7634.

Your California privacy rights

If you are a California resident you may request to know what personal information we have collected about you, the categories of sources, the purposes for collecting it, and the categories of third parties with whom we share it.

You may request deletion or correction of personal information, subject to exceptions for information we are required to retain, including medical and billing records.

You may opt out of the sharing of personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

You will not be discriminated against for exercising any of these rights.

To make a request, call 925-969-7634 or email info@assistoncallprof.com. We will verify your identity before responding.

Cookies and analytics

This website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences and understand how the site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect how parts of the site work.

Data retention and security

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide services and to meet legal, clinical and accreditation record-retention requirements, and we apply administrative, physical and technical safeguards to protect it.

Children’s privacy

This website is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information online from children under 13.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The revised version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
