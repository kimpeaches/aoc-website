Our home health services

Every service we provide, in full.

Select any service to read what it covers. All services are provided under a physician-ordered plan of care and coordinated by our clinical team.

Our skilled nursing services are delivered by licensed nurses who bring hospital-level clinical expertise into the home. Every episode of care begins with a thorough assessment of the patient’s condition, medications, home environment and support system, so the plan of care reflects exactly what that person needs.

From complex wound management to disease-specific teaching, our nurses coordinate closely with the patient’s physician and family. The goal is always the same: keep the patient safe, comfortable and improving at home, and reduce avoidable trips back to the hospital.

What this service includes

  • Initial comprehensive evaluation visit to the patient.
  • Provide skilled nursing services that require more specialised nursing care, which include, but are not limited to:
  • Wound care treatment
  • Wound Vac
  • Patient and caregiver teaching on disease management, but not limited to teaching about diabetes and insulin administration, congestive heart failure, hypertension, other cardiovascular diseases, and other body systems
  • Respiratory equipment teaching and management
  • Colostomy care
  • Catheter care (Foley / Supra-Pubic Catheter)
  • Injections and lab work (only in relation to skilled nursing needs)
  • Medication Management

Laboratory draws performed in relation to skilled nursing needs include:

PT / INR Blood Glucose CBC Chemistry Profile Digoxin levels Theophylline levels Dilantin levels

Our home health aides provide the hands-on, everyday help that makes independent living possible. Working under the supervision of a nurse and within the physician-ordered plan of care, aides assist with the personal tasks that have become difficult or unsafe to manage alone.

Beyond the practical help, aides become a familiar, reassuring presence in the home. They notice changes in appetite, mobility and mood, and report them to the clinical team so small problems are addressed before they become serious ones.

What this service includes

  • Assist patients with personal care needs.
  • Plan and prepare meals and assist in feeding patients as directed.
Request this service

Our physical therapists help patients recover strength, balance and confidence after surgery, injury, stroke or a period of decline. Therapy takes place in the patient’s own home, using their real stairs, doorways and furniture — which makes progress directly transferable to daily life.

Each programme starts with an evaluation of strength, range of motion, gait and fall risk, then progresses at a pace the patient can sustain. Families are taught how to assist safely so gains continue between visits.

What this service includes

  • Provide evaluation and treatment of the patient’s rehabilitation needs.
  • Therapeutic exercises for muscle conditioning and strengthening.
  • Educate patients and families about safety.
Request this service

Occupational therapy focuses on the tasks that fill an ordinary day — dressing, bathing, cooking, writing, getting in and out of a chair. Our therapists break those activities down, rebuild the underlying skills, and adapt the approach or the environment so the patient can do more for themselves.

Where ability cannot be fully restored, adaptive equipment fills the gap. Our therapists recommend and train patients on the tools and techniques that restore genuine independence rather than dependence on others.

What this service includes

  • Provide training for activities of daily living.
  • Skills and techniques on adaptive equipment.
Request this service

Our speech-language pathologists treat difficulties with speech, language, voice, cognition and swallowing — problems that commonly follow a stroke, a neurological diagnosis or prolonged hospitalisation. Restoring communication restores a person’s ability to take part in their own care and their own family life.

Swallowing safety is equally central. Evaluation and treatment of dysphagia reduce the risk of aspiration and pneumonia, and give families clear, practical guidance on diet textures, positioning and pacing at mealtimes.

What this service includes

  • Swallowing evaluation and treatment.
  • Speech and language evaluation and treatment.
  • Cognitive retraining.
Request this service

Illness is never only medical. Our medical social workers help patients and families work through the social, emotional and financial pressures that surround a serious diagnosis or a change in ability — from insurance and benefits questions to caregiver strain and long-term planning.

They also connect households to the wider network of community resources available to them. By assessing needs and coordinating those services, our social workers advocate for your needs and help you make informed decisions about your care.

What this service includes

  • Assist patients and families in dealing with social, emotional and financial concerns related to the patient’s condition.
  • Assess the patient’s home situation and support system.
  • Coordinate and refer to available community resources.
  • Provide counselling, advocacy and long-term care planning assistance.
Request this service

A plan of care only works if the right equipment, medication and test results are in place at the right time. Assist on Call arranges the durable medical equipment a patient needs at home, along with the training to use it safely and confidently.

We also coordinate pharmaceutical and laboratory services so prescriptions and diagnostic results move quickly between the patient, the physician and the clinical team — without the family having to chase them.

What this service includes

  • Provide durable medical equipment and medical supplies needed in the home.
  • Instruct the patient and caregivers on the safe use and maintenance of equipment.
  • Coordinate pharmaceutical services and medication delivery.
  • Arrange laboratory services and communicate results to the physician.

Placement referrals

When home is no longer the safest option.

Choosing a residential care setting is one of the hardest decisions a family makes. Assist on Call helps you understand the options, compare them honestly, and find a placement that genuinely suits your loved one’s medical needs, personality and budget.

How we help

  • Assess the level of care your loved one currently requires.
  • Explain the differences between assisted living, residential care homes and skilled nursing facilities.
  • Refer you to vetted communities and care homes that match those needs.
  • Help coordinate the transition so nothing falls through the cracks.
  • Continue to provide private duty or home health support where appropriate.
